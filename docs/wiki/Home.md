# Welcome to the seeWallpaper community

Create an animated wallpaper, share it with the community, and keep your creator name in the catalogue.

**[Submit a wallpaper](https://github.com/Hytachi182/seeWallpapers/issues/new?template=template_submission.yml)** · **[Get seeWallpaper](https://github.com/Hytachi182/seeWallpapers/releases/latest)**

## Start here

- [Create a template](https://github.com/Hytachi182/seeWallpapers/blob/main/docs/wiki/Create-a-template.md): folder structure, manifest, SDK, and testing.
- [Share your template](https://github.com/Hytachi182/seeWallpapers/blob/main/docs/wiki/Share-your-template.md): upload a ZIP with a screenshot. No Git or fork required.
- [Catalogue and review](https://github.com/Hytachi182/seeWallpapers/blob/main/docs/wiki/Catalogue-and-review.md): what happens after submission and how updates work.

Creating and submitting a wallpaper requires no payment. To use the submission form, sign in to a GitHub account.

The wiki is maintained by the project maintainer. Community creators submit their wallpapers through the form; they do not need permission to edit the wiki or push to `main`.
