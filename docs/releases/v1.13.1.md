# seeWallpaper 1.13.1

This animation revision updates Inkbound Courier, Satin Afterglow and Rose Riviera to template version 1.0.1. Their original illustrations are preserved; motion belongs to the surrounding scene materials rather than facial or body deformation.

- **Inkbound Courier:** source-texture refraction animates the harbor, distant coastal birds replace floating ink trails, and petals follow a shared breeze at three depths. Harbor water has its own toggle.
- **Satin Afterglow:** lantern flames lean and flicker at the existing candle locations, their warm light varies with the flame, and rose petals follow coherent gusts instead of isolated paths.
- **Rose Riviera:** continuous one-source-pixel water refraction replaces broad displaced strips, pool caustics move coherently, a separate coastal water region animates, lantern flames flicker, and a soft car-bonnet highlight replaces floating star-shaped glints.

All three scenes bundle an identical offline `motion.js` helper. A requestAnimationFrame clock respects 15/30/60 FPS budgets without counting a time remainder twice, and repeated starts keep one animation loop. The scene controls, host pause/resume, visibility suspension, speed zero and reduced motion remain supported.

## Validation

The shared scene capture passed desktop/portrait rendering, each setting and pause/resume for all three themes. Their existing regression checks passed stable character pixels, reduced motion and zero speed; the two manga scenes also passed 24-hour previews, bounded 4K rendering and offline loading. The new scheduling check passed 15/30/60 FPS, elapsed-time speed, repeated starts and inactive suspension. Updated `.seewall` archives passed CRC and source-byte equality checks.

Four-second live recordings and 1/2/4-second comparison frames were captured before and after the revision. Review of those frames and desktop/portrait captures found no visible water-boundary seam, misplaced flame or character deformation. Still-frame review does not establish perceived smoothness during continuous playback or performance across GPUs. Actual desktop attachment, physical-monitor behavior and local installer/portable verification were not rerun for this animation-only preparation.
