# seeWallpaper 1.12.0

Four new offline scenes bring the catalogue to 55 wallpapers: Burrow Battle, Rose Riviera, Velvet Circuit and Copper Current.

Multi-monitor placement now uses fresh physical-pixel Windows geometry for independent wallpapers, duplication and panoramas. Desktop-coordinate conversion happens after Explorer attachment so differing DPI contexts do not rescale or displace the next wallpaper. A reported setup showed 2880 x 1620 instead of Windows' 1920 x 1080; the fix removes the DPI-dependent detection/placement path that could cause that discrepancy.

Five repeatable functional scenarios exercise the actual assignment service, Explorer wallpaper windows and WebView output on three monitors, with isolated saved state. They cover correct scenes and edge coverage, targeted replacement/removal, failed replacement preservation, clone/span transitions, restoration, caller DPI modes and simulated disconnect/reconnect. [Details and commands](../multi-display-scaling.md#repeatable-functional-tests).

Local validation: 178 existing .NET tests, five three-monitor functional scenarios against a source build and an extracted portable test ZIP, 42 rendered captures and 336 edge samples per functional run. The release-automation suite passed 24 tests. Burrow Battle and Velvet Circuit passed twenty-minute gameplay simulations; all four scenes passed Edge desktop/portrait, settings and pause/resume checks. The user's exact three-Full-HD scaling setup and physical dock/unplug/sleep transitions still require on-device verification. Desktop functional tests are opt-in and are not exercised by ordinary hosted CI runners.

## Changes since v1.11.0

Built-in catalogue: **55 wallpapers**.

### New wallpapers

- **Burrow Battle** (`burrow-battle`): An original self-playing artillery battle between two teams of tiny burrowers on floating meadow islands. Wind-driven rockets and grenades carve real craters, blast opponents into the sea, and decide alternating turns. Health, team scores and automatic rematches. Original procedural cartoon art, entirely offline.
- **Copper Current** (`copper-current`): An original macro circuit-board wallpaper: deep green solder mask, copper traces, plated vias, graphite chips, metal pins and miniature electronic components. Gentle luminous signals follow the routed traces and status LEDs breathe softly. Procedural artwork, customizable illumination and entirely offline.
- **Rose Riviera** (`rose-riviera`): An original rose-pink fashion miniature overlooking the sea: a curved coastal villa, turquoise pool, tropical garden, pearlescent convertible and original adult fashion figurines. Animated water reflections, drifting bougainvillea petals, champagne lights and delicate glints. Generated original artwork, entirely offline.
- **Velvet Circuit** (`velvet-circuit`): An original self-playing pinball machine in an atmospheric arcade. A chrome ball rolls under gravity, collides with rails, posts, pop bumpers and slingshots, and is struck by reactive mechanical flippers. Automatic spring launches, three-ball games, scoring, target banks and persistent session high scores. Procedural perspective rendering with lacquer, metal and glass materials, entirely offline.
