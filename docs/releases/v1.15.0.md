# seeWallpaper 1.15.0

The catalogue grows from 61 to 65 wallpapers with four original, code-drawn and offline scenes: Midnight Drive, Lofi Hideaway, Snowglow Village and Stormwatch Lighthouse. A follow-up motion audit strengthens three quiet scenes.

## Animation changes

- Midnight Drive follows a fictional sports coupe along a city highway, with distance-driven wheel rotation, suspension, paint reflections, headlight beams, tail light trails, traffic, wet asphalt and optional night and rain.
- Lofi Hideaway shows a cozy night room with a breathing sleeping cat, rain sliding down the window, a spinning record with floating notes, a steaming mug, a desk lamp, fairy lights and passing car lights.
- Snowglow Village is a Christmas village with a decorated tree, a toy train projected around it on a circular track, chasing roof lights, chimney smoke, a snowman and Santa's sleigh crossing the moon.
- Stormwatch Lighthouse adds a rotating lantern beam, five swell layers, spray bursts on the rocks, forked lightning, slanted rain and a fishing boat riding the swell. Lightning flashes are brief, several seconds apart and can be turned off.
- The [follow-up motion audit](../motion-audit.md) strengthens Firefly Grove (looping fireflies, breathing light shafts, mist and swaying grass), Sakura Night (canopy breeze, drifting clouds, petal gusts) and Winter Snowfall (chimney smoke, fireplace glow, valley mist, branch snow, new chalet toggle).
- Sakura Night's change lives in the shared scene runtime. AI Core, Data Tunnel, Neural Network, Operations Center and Rainy Window receive that runtime with a patch version; their own motion is unchanged.

All new artwork is drawn in code, with no images, fonts or external requests.

## Validation

- Local Release build succeeded; 208 .NET tests passed and three optional physical-desktop tests were skipped. The 24 release-automation tests passed.
- Each new scene has a dedicated check script covering core motion with optional effects disabled, settings, reduced motion, zero speed, pause, 24-hour preview, 4K render bounding, portrait layout and offline loading. Preview capture verified that every setting has a visible effect on all four.
- Motion audit percentages are a screening heuristic from short rendered samples, not an animation-quality or FPS benchmark. The five shared-runtime scenes were re-sampled with unchanged motion and no errors.

Local installer/portable lifecycle tests and physical desktop or multi-monitor tests were not rerun for this preparation. Continuous playback quality and performance across GPUs are not established by still-frame review. Downloads remain on the published stable release until this PR is merged and Windows release packaging completes.
