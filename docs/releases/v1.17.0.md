## Changes since v1.16.0

Built-in catalogue: **75 wallpapers**.

### New wallpapers

- **Bioluminescent Tide** (`bioluminescent-tide`): A moonless beach where the surf comes alive with light. Each wave rolls in from the horizon and lights up as it breaks, peeling along the crest, then runs up the wet sand and leaves glittering sparks as it drains away. A night walker leaves footprints that flash and ripple, jellyfish pulse beneath the swell, spray glows against a rocky headland, a fishing boat's lantern bobs on the horizon and the Milky Way arches overhead. Selectable glow colour. Code-drawn, entirely offline.
- **Drifting Jellyfish** (`drifting-jellyfish`): Jellyfish of every size pulse and drift across the whole screen through deep blue water. Bells contract and relax, ruffled rims and glowing organs shine, long tentacles and frilly arms trail and sway behind them, near ones pass slowly in front while small distant ones glow in the haze, marine snow sinks through slow shafts of light. Adjustable number of jellyfish, glow and tint colour. Code-drawn, entirely offline.
- **Glasshouse Conservatory** (`glasshouse-conservatory`): Inside a Victorian glasshouse on a rainy afternoon. Raindrops run down the curved panes of the iron-ribbed vault while rain falls beyond the glass, palms, tree ferns, banana plants, monstera and flowering shrubs line a tiled aisle, a small fountain splashes and ripples, butterflies flutter between the beds, mist drifts up from the soil, sunbeams break through between showers and pendant lamps glow warmer towards dusk. Code-drawn, entirely offline.
- **Liftoff Dawn** (`liftoff-dawn`): A rocket waits on a seaside launch pad at dawn. The countdown runs, the engines ignite in a billow of smoke, the rocket climbs on a long exhaust trail through the clouds while the camera follows, drops its first stage and vanishes into the stars, then the next vehicle rolls out. Adjustable time of day. Code-drawn, entirely offline.
- **Night Train Viaduct** (`night-train-viaduct`): A night train crosses a tall stone viaduct above a moonlit river. A steam locomotive with turning wheels and side rods pulls carriages whose lit windows show passengers, its headlamp sweeps the rails ahead and a plume of steam trails behind, a signal turns red as it passes, and a mountain village glows on the hillside under the stars. Optional snowfall and blue hour. Code-drawn, entirely offline.
- **Pocket Quest** (`pocket-quest`): A handheld console plays a monster-collecting adventure all by itself. The hero walks out of the village, wades through tall grass and a wild creature appears: the screen flashes, a turn-based battle plays out with menus, attacks, health bars and typed messages, then the next encounter ends with a throw of the ball. Four screen palettes, pixel grid and room glow. Every sprite, tile and letter is drawn in code, entirely offline.
