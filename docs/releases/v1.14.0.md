# seeWallpaper 1.14.0

This preparation supersedes the unpublished 1.13.1 draft and includes its manga and Rose Riviera animation fixes. The catalogue grows from 57 to 61 wallpapers with four original, offline scenes: Stratos Flight, Emberwatch Knight, Hollow Lantern and Gilded Court.

## Animation changes

- Stratos Flight animates aircraft independently of clouds, with flight paths, banking and engine effects.
- Emberwatch Knight adds articulated knight motion, cloth, embers and atmospheric lighting.
- Hollow Lantern adds drifting ghosts, lantern light and seasonal atmosphere.
- Gilded Court adds an animated sovereign and marching guards with grounded foot placement, banners, lights and court atmosphere.
- The [61-theme motion audit](../motion-audit.md) strengthens Moonlit Dunes, Satin Afterglow, Inkbound Courier, Pirate Cove and Lunar Silence. Sand advection, city haze and lantern reflections, harbor water and birds, sea flow, and a lunar rover make those scenes more visibly active. Character silhouettes and established compositions are retained.
- The earlier revision of Rose Riviera, Inkbound Courier and Satin Afterglow retains local material effects and host-aware frame scheduling. Satin's final revision avoids river refraction to preserve bridge and roof geometry.

Artwork is original and bundled locally, with provenance records and no franchise characters or external asset requests. This records the source workflow, not legal clearance.

## Validation

- Local Release build succeeded without warnings or errors; 204 .NET tests passed and three optional physical-desktop tests were skipped.
- The new scenes have dedicated offline, portrait, 24-hour preview, zero-speed, reduced-motion and bounded-render checks. The five audit improvements passed pause/resume and motion-preference regression checks.
- The audit used rendered browser samples and desktop/portrait captures. Pixel-change percentages are a screening heuristic, not an animation-quality or FPS benchmark.
- Offline scene archives include every dependency and pass CRC and source-byte equality checks.

Local installer/portable lifecycle tests and physical desktop tests were not rerun for this preparation. Continuous playback quality and performance across GPUs are not established by still-frame review. Downloads remain on the published stable release until this PR is merged and Windows release packaging completes.
