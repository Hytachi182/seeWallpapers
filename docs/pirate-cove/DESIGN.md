# Design System: Pirate Cove

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Pirate Cove"**

A painted coastal adventure scene with an original character overlooking a sunset harbor.

The existing illustration and bundled fabric-mask provenance remain unchanged; no new image assets were introduced.

## Typography

No new text overlay or HUD is introduced.

## Layout

The existing cover crop uses a 90% horizontal focus in portrait, retaining the foreground character.

## Elevation & Depth

Actual source-row sea texture refraction remains inside established channel masks. Gull passages are more frequent; existing fabric masks, lights and waterfalls remain.

**The Safe Channel Rule.** Keep water refraction inside established safe channels. Preserve the head and neck, and retain artwork-derived fabric masks and their anchors.

## Components

### Offline scene

Keep existing host settings, pause/resume, visibility and frame-rate controls. Reduced motion and speed zero suspend animation. All assets remain local.

The [catalogue motion audit](../motion-audit.md) records short-window pixel changes, a screening heuristic rather than a quality or FPS measure. Desktop/portrait captures establish inspected composition; continuous playback and physical desktop performance were not reviewed.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** preserve established scene materials and motion boundaries.
- **Don't** add whole-image zoom to inflate motion measurements.
