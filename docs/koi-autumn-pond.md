# Koi Autumn Pond

A Japanese garden pond seen from above in golden autumn light, in **Nature**. Koi swim slow, looping paths across the pond. Each body follows the path it has just swum, with a tail beat that runs down the spine, swept pectoral and pelvic fins, and a forked translucent tail. The nine varieties (kohaku, yamabuki, showa, sanke, tancho, orange ogon, asagi, platinum and chagoi) each have their own pattern. Koi drift between the surface and deeper water, where they shrink a little, fade into the green and cast a shorter shadow on the pebbled floor. Every few seconds one rises to sip at the surface, which leaves a ripple and a small splash.

Maple leaves fall from the overhanging branch. They look large and blurred near the viewer, tumble and flutter as they fall, and cast a shadow that closes in on the leaf as it nears the water. When a leaf lands it ripples the surface, then drifts and slowly sinks. Sun rays and caustic flecks move over the floor, and the sky's reflection shimmers on the water. Lily pads with lotus flowers bob gently, the canopy sways and casts a dappled shadow, and a red wooden bridge shades the koi that swim beneath it, between mossy stones and two stone lanterns.

Settings control animation speed, the number of koi (3–9), how many maple leaves fall, a dusk mode (a blue evening grade with glowing stone lanterns and fireflies), light rain (raindrop ripples and streaks), and the underwater sun rays. Clicking or tapping the water, when the host forwards input, makes a ripple. Everything is drawn in code: no images, fonts or network requests. The floor, banks, bridge and canopy are drawn to off-screen layers once per resize, so each frame redraws only the moving parts.

Portrait screens move the bridge, canopy, banks and lily pads, and shrink the koi slightly. Host pause/resume, visibility and frame-rate callbacks are supported. A speed of zero freezes the scene; the reduced-motion preference shows a still frame. The render buffer is bounded to 1920 × 1080.

`index.html?preview=12` generates a deterministic still. `build/check-koi-autumn-pond.mjs` verifies core motion with leaves, rain and rays disabled, settings including invalid values, reduced motion, zero speed, pause, 4K bounding, portrait layout and offline loading. `build/capture-template-previews.mjs` checks real Edge rendering and that each setting has a visible effect.
