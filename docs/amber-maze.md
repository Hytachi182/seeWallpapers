# Amber Maze

An original autonomous maze chase in **Games**. A lantern robot collects shards while three angular sentinels patrol or chase it. Four overcharge crystals give eight seconds of protection and allow the robot to disable pursuers for four seconds. Completing a maze generates a new one; losing three lives restarts the game. Shards already collected stay cleared after losing a life.

All maps are generated by a spanning maze with extra junctions for evasion. A weighted shortest-path autopilot seeks remaining collectibles and penalizes routes near sentinels. Player and enemy moves are interpolated along corridors; same-tile and opposite-direction crossing collisions count. The HUD reports real score, remaining shards, lives, level, power time and flight count. Best-score storage is optional.

Originality: code-drawn lantern robot, diamond sentinels, copper maze edges, shard collectibles and generated layouts. No Pac-Man character, ghost sprites, branded maps, logos, sounds or third-party assets are included. The theme uses the general maze-chase genre.

Visual direction: restrained copper and amber lights against slate corridors, small mint overcharge crystals and optional fading footsteps. The maze stays central and fits landscape or portrait displays. Settings control accent color, speed, illuminated edges, footsteps and telemetry. The host pause/resume, visibility and frame-rate callbacks are supported. Reduced-motion preference initially shows a still scene. Physics uses fixed steps; the art buffer is bounded for large screens.

`index.html?preview=12` produces a seeded snapshot (stored best score may differ). The existing template content glob includes this folder; a `.seewall` package can be imported into an installed application. Validation tools: `build/check-amber-maze.mjs` for gameplay and `build/capture-template-previews.mjs` for the real Edge renderer and host lifecycle.
